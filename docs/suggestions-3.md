# Suggestions after the 2026-10-04/05 balance pass

Collected from every agent's report (census, win conditions, raids, hives, pacing, conquest, retreat, weight, trade,
work priorities, loot, hunting, hidden sites, survey, water, tiles). Nothing here is built. Pick by number.

Final census (20 autopilot games, 2 years, current dev): 2 of 20 fell (both Cautious), 1 coalition victory
(Aggressive), 0 hive victories, 17 still going. Horde attacks climb from ~4.5 per 100 days early to ~20–25 late;
group raids ~6–11 per 100 days; outsider raids grow from 0 to ~13 per 100 days. Deaths: field fights 36%, raids 20%,
hordes at bases 16%, thirst 4% (was the top killer).

## Difficulty and win conditions
1. **The autopilot now survives too easily?** 85% of games last 2 years (this morning: 2%). Either keep it (the
   autopilot is now a far better player than a new human) or raise late pressure: late attack hordes grow with the day
   so they test the walls instead of mostly being held.
2. **Hive victory is still rare:** Aggressive destroys ~3.7 hives and ~3.7 new ones are built in 2 years. Options:
   new hives only spread from clusters of 2+, or a hive's guard and income shrink when a neighbour dies, so clusters
   unravel once you start winning.
3. **Outposts get overrun:** when a horde reaches an outpost it breaks in more than half the time. Give outposts a
   defence plan (the autopilot leaves a small garrison and walls before expanding), especially captured bases, which
   now get razed easily.
4. **Razing and late gangs feel harsh?** Lower the raze odds (raiders 22%) or the settling rate (3% a day).
5. **Cautious falls most.** Its tribute habit and small teams still bleed it; a "turtle" bonus (stronger walls when
   few people go out) would make playing safe viable.

## Pacing and decisions
6. **Standing road orders:** trips auto-answer storms, supply trucks, patrols and road hordes and report once at
   home (cards a day 2.4 → ~1.8; stops the game pausing for obvious answers).
7. **Perk points stop pausing the clock** (a badge on the roster instead of a card).
8. **A card when the dead start a new hive** ("the dead are massing near X": break it up / mark it / ignore), and a
   rumour when a hive site is picked. Players currently never see a hive being born.
9. **A card when a group turns hostile** (envoy with a gift, ask allies, prepare).
10. **A winter card:** "The stores won't last the winter": ration now, plan food runs, trade, close the gate.
11. **A pacing director:** no small cards within 6 hours of a crisis, at most 2 road events per team a day.
12. **Fix the one-answer cards:** sheltering from a storm, helping stranded traders, "Prepare defences" vs "Hold".

## Combat
13. **Retreat in auto-resolved fights** (most autopilot fights are settled by the numbers and have no retreat).
14. **Enemies throw molotovs and pipe bombs too.**
15. **Let units move to the edge for a turn before the AI sounds the retreat.**

## Economy and trade
16. **Groups use or issue the gear on their shelves** so prices recover over time.
17. **Radio rumours of who wants what** ("the Iron Jackals are paying well for fuel").
18. **Late-game scrap shortage:** cheaper well repairs or parts-for-scrap filters (hubs wrecked by raids die of thirst).
19. **A "ration water" rule** like short food rations; water runs by truck.

## Exploration and the map
20. **A "known area" overlay** shading tiles already surveyed; the autopilot surveys in quiet stretches only.
21. **Draw ruins on the map** and a **coalition territory overlay**.
22. **Dedicated bike sprite** for teams riding on the map.

## People, work and hunting
23. **Stewards assign roles and tune priorities** instead of only managing Manual people.
24. **Crews leave from outposts**, not only the hub.
25. **Animal threats** (wolves, bears) on hunts; hides give scrap; fresh meat lifts mood.
26. **A per-team loot limit** ("stop picking up at Heavy"); heavily loaded teams are noisier.
