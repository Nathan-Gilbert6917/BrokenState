## Base tiers

Camp, shelter, outpost and hub, every yard 76 × 76 to start (see [The grid](#the-grid)). Camps can be set up, struck and upgraded [staging].

### Where a base may stand
- The user's requests 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23: "The ban should be on the tiles for town and city crawls". No base of yours goes up on a town's or city's crawl tile (its centre, a bare city block: citycrawl crawlTile; checked by staging campProblem, parties missionProblem, startpick CRAWL_WHY).
- The suburbs and a city tile with a place of its own take a base once the dead there are cleared (see *Clearing the spot of the dead*).
- The start still keeps START_CLEAR tiles from any town or city (startpick isSettlementTile).

Rules:

- **Isometric site view**: the tile's whole map with its land and nothing built (basemap openSiteView: buildBaseMap site, from a stand-in camp on the books only while it is made; b.site). Opened by main.js tidyUi through ui/campsite.js siteViewDue; the clock is held as by any battle screen.

The open waits for the box to paint; asked again meanwhile nothing more is queued (siteOpening), and it looks again when it runs. The clock waits while the box is up (main.js blocked: generatingUp). `tools/campspottest.js` (section 2).
- The **5 × 5** under the mouse is drawn with the fire in its middle cell, green where it can go, red where not, the picked one gold with "Camp fire here" (siteGhostAt, ui/isoview.js). A click picks (siteClickAt).

- Not too near the edge (basemap SITE_RANGE) nor on the river (siteProblem). The governor and a deferred pick get the game's own (defaultSite).

### Setup time and yield
- Time = the camp fire's CAMP.fireHours (1 work-hour) + clearing every piece with a cell in the 5 × 5 (basescatter SITE / siteBox, CLEAR hours). Shared by the team, a work-hour an hour each, to the quarter hour (staging siteSetup / campPitchHours; the pitching task's hours set again when the spot is picked; parties pitchHoursFor for the planner).
- **No axe, saw or machete on the team** (teamChopTool): trees, logs and stumps take CLEAR.NO_TOOL as long and give only SITE.NO_TOOL_WOOD (a quarter) of their wood (basescatter siteGet).
- Tent bags no longer shorten setup. A band's tarps on the road stay CAMP.tarpHours (tents pitchHours, bandPitchSaved).
- **The time said is the spot's own** (the user's report 2026-10-23: "it said 30 minutes and it did not finish for 9 in game hours there was no trees or rocks just and open 5x5 on a hill"). The order's text and the arrival line give the game's pick's time as that ("the game's pick takes 30 minutes; a spot on a hill, in a hollow or among trees takes longer"). Picking a spot logs its own time and work ("sets up camp on its spot …: 2 h 30 min (the camp fire, digging 6 cells down level)", 7 work-hours for 3: staging pickSite / setupLine / setupWhat / levelWords; autoSite's log too). The picker names the levelling apart from the clearing ("6 work-hours digging 6 cells down level": basemap siteYield dig, ui/campsite setupWords). `tools/campspottest.js` (section 1, 2).
- While it goes up the State Map shows a bar over the tile ("Setting up camp 67%": ui/render.js pitchPct, the parties' pitching task). It moves with the clock's minutes (hourFrac counted on the hours worked; the user's report 2026-10-26: "The setting up camp progress bar did not move as time went on it stayed at 0% until the camp was done and set": the tick credits whole hours, so a setup under an hour read 0% to the end).
- The camp stands at that spot (base.plan.origin, plan.fresh: basemap originOf, gridBox, freshBase). Its 5 × 5 round the fire is cleared and the yield goes to its stores with a log line (basemap clearCampSite, siteYield). The land all round is the tile's own (basescatter landSpec origin / fresh); the rest is cleared as needed.

### A camp on a hill stands on it, and buildings stand on the hills
- The user's requests 2026-10-23: (a screenshot of a camp dug down into a hill, its banks all round) "Allow building on a hill setting up a camp on a hill burrowed down into the hill"; then "It should not be trying to burrow just build on the hill as if it was the ground floor and only allow building the camp if the 5x5 fits on the hill in whole otherwise set it to red".
- **The camp's 5 × 5 whole on one level**: a spot whose cells are not all at one height is refused, red in the picker ("Over a hill's edge: the camp's square has to stand whole on one level": basemap siteProblem); the game's pick, a blind camp and Let the game pick keep to such spots (siteinfo bestSiteOn onOneLevel). On a hill it stands on the hill: no digging, the camp fire's work alone on open ground. The camp keeps its level (base.plan.level: basescatter siteLevel at makeCamp; basemap levelOf; 0 for level ground and bases from before). The levelling words and costs (siteYield dig, levelWords) are left for the edge case of a land changed under a spot.
- **What is cleared keeps the ground's height** (base.land.keepH on a camp set up since: basescatter landOf): a tree felled on a hill leaves the hill. Before, the ground went with it, dug down to the plain's (old bases keep that).
- **Buildings on any level ground, its ground floor there**: the hills and hollows themselves (rise, dip) are never in a building's way (basemap landUnder; a spring's pond still is); a building, a pen, a crop plot, a prop stands where its cells and the cells outside its doors are all at one height, else "Over a hill's edge: it has to stand whole on one level" (groundWhy, UNEVEN_WHY; placeProblemAt, so the game's own layout too). Walls, gates, wall stands and the defences follow the ground a cell at a time. A dry hollow's floor is built on as it is (a wet one waits: ponds pondWhy).
- **The map** is laid from the camp's level (layLand lift; F.lv, the map's datum: combat mapLv): the camp's ground at c.h 0, the land round it higher or lower, the river's water at the plain's level (c.wet deep, wl), the crag's height less the lift. What the base puts on ground off that level stands on it (basemap groundBuilt): c.ter and c.h the ground's (drawn on its column, its floor's colour: ui/isoview terrainPrims), or for a loft, a wall stand, a watchtower its own height over the ground (c.gh the ground, c.h both: isoview groundLv, combat losTop); c.bld keeps ponds out of a building in a hollow (ponds pondsBattle).
- **Heights from level ground** where they mean the land's: sight (combat sightH; ui/combat sightAt), the hover's hill and hollow lines (not a building's floor), the AI's liking for high ground (highground terrainScore), hollows (highground sunk), ponds (c.h + mapLv < 0, wl back in the map's levels). Line of sight, climbing and vehicles compare heights on the one map.
- The picker's facts are from the spot's level (siteinfo gridsOf: higher, lower and sight layers a level each; ui/campsite Ground: "Level, on a hill, 1 level up: the camp stands on it").
- Neither Establish base nor Build outpost queues its step up (see *Step ups are buildings*; the guide's Place the shelter step says so: ui/guide).
- Modules: [basescatter siteLevel / landOf keepH / layLand lift, basemap siteProblem / landUnder / groundWhy / groundBuilt / levelOf, combat mapLv / sightH, highground, ponds, staging makeCamp, ui/guide].
- `tools/campspottest.js` (section 1: an open hilltop, a spot over a hill's edge refused; section 6: a camp on a hilltop, built on on the hill and on the plain, never over the edge, its map); `tools/terraformtest.js`, `tools/terrainweathertest.js` (a hollow's floor built on as it is).

### What a camp's spot is like
- The user's words 2026-10-23: "add valuable information to setting up camp. Update let the game pick".
- One set of facts for a 5 × 5, read by the picker and the game's own pick (siteinfo siteInfo / landFacts): the team's setup time and yield with and without an axe; the ground (level, a hill dug down, a hollow filled in); the lie round the levelled camp (ring 4–10 cells: looks down on it / overlooked; crag counts as higher); sight (open share within 12 cells); room (open share in the 24 × 24, roomClear hours) and how far a yard grows before the edge; wood within 20 cells; the river, the tile's water (waterSource) and the road; places the team knows of (knownPois) with distance and direction; the dead and their kinds (siteclear siteThreat, per tile).
- The game's pick (siteinfo bestSite / bestSiteOn / siteScore, SITE_SCORE; basemap defaultSite calls it, so Let the game pick, autoSite, the governor and a team with nobody to ask use it): setup work-hours −1 each, high ground +6, sight +4, river +3 (within 20 cells, 0 at 80), road +2 (0 at 40), room +8, wood +4 (2400 = full), room to the edge +6, the dead −0.6 each; summed-area quick scores for every spot (gridsOf), the best 24 in full; never the river or crag, off buildings and high ground while it can; ties nearest the middle; deterministic, cached by landSpec (3 lands kept). Scored as if with a cutting tool, so the pick does not move with inventory.
- Shown (ui/campsite siteFacts / siteFactLine / sitePanel / siteHoverText; ui/isoview): the panel's coloured fact grid and "The game picked this spot because …" (pickReasons / pickWords) on the game's spot; the hover line's short facts; "The game's pick" tagged on the map while another spot is picked (siteGhostAt gameAt); autoSite logs why. `tools/campspottest.js` (section 5).

### Attacks on a camp by the dead nearby
- The user's request 2026-10-26 (the heat epic #64 §5, issue #69): "Higher zombie and Raider activity on the map and tile means more attacks against your camp. So you are incentivized to reduce the activity of the zombies and raiders in the area starting with the POI ones and later the attraction of zombies and raiders from the tile as a whole. … if there is a high level of zombies in a tile and you reduced a POI to no zombies by killing them loudly you will get a lot more zombies trickling in to the map as turns go by."
- Every camp and base, yours and the AI groups' homes (campattack campAttackHour, hourly from staging campsHour → campscout groundsHour), rolls hash dice (seed, tile or base, hour) for three kinds of attack. The tunables are one block, `CAMP_ATTACK`.
  - **The grounds** (per base): the dead left on its own grounds (groundsLeft: `base.grounds.dead`, a blind camp's pool or a POI camp's from #70): `GROUNDS_P` 1/30 an hour at 10 or more left, × left / 10 below (at least × 0.4).
  - **The tile's trickle** (per tile, shared): the open ground's dead not on any base's grounds (trickleFree = openDead − the grounds living in the open ground): `TRICKLE_P` 1/12 × fill³ an hour, fill = trickleFree / the tile's cap. At the base's × 1: a tile at 75% about every 28 h, 45% every 5–6 days, 28% (the top of low) every 3 weeks, 15% every 5 months, 0 never. A starting hub (seeds 1–44: a highway tile at about 27%) about every 3–4 weeks.
  - **Raiders** (per tile, shared; camps and outposts only, never a main hub, yours or a group's: the hostile groups' raids are what come at those): a band from the tile's raiders (a POI camp's POI first): `RAID_P` 1/80 × (raiders / raiderCap)³ an hour: at the cap about every 3 days, 70% every 10 days, 45% every 5 weeks, a low level hardly ever (a starting hub: about every 3 months). Never in the same hour as a wave of the dead, nor within 72 h of the last band at that base (`RAID_COOLDOWN`). Bandits pick soft targets: × 14 ÷ the base's defence (factions baseDefense; an AI home: 3 × its by-the-numbers defence), between 0.1 and 1. The tile's noise draws the dead, not raiders. The hostile groups' raids (factions launchRaid) and the hives' hordes are unchanged.
- **The multipliers** (attackMult): night × 1.5, day × 0.8 (the dead only); the tile's noise memory (deadpop noiseOf) × (1 + noise / 25), at most × 3; the base's own Heat (hordes baseHeat; an AI home: half its people) × (0.75 + 0.025 × Heat) between 0.6 and 2; running silent × 0.7; camouflage netting × 0.8. No attack within `COOLDOWN` 6 h of the last, to an empty base, during a fight, or while its card waits.
- **Several bases on one tile** (one per POI and one normal base, #70) share the trickle and the raiders: one roll for the tile at the loudest base's multiplier, the wave to that base. Each base keeps its own grounds' roll.
- **The wave** (waveSource, the one function #71 replaces with the Not cleared rooms): the grounds' part first (3–8, +4 over 30 left), then the trickle's (2 + 3% of trickleFree, × up to 1.5 on a loud tile, ± 1, at most 10) drawn **out of** the open ground (deadpop drawFromTile) as the wave forms; a raider band of 2–5 (2 + 3 × the raider fill, ± 1) drawn out (drawRaiders; a POI's via killRaiders, quietly). At most 20 (a battle's most).
- **The fight**: a camp or shelter gets the card "The dead come out of the fields at <base>" (or "Raiders found <base>"), with "Nearby: the dead medium, raiders low": Defend the camp (the base defence battle, handler `campAttack`) or keep out of their way (food and meds × 0.9; hiding from raiders: food, meds and scrap × 0.85). A base past the camp stage whose defences hold the wave (defences holdCheck) cuts the dead down at the walls by the numbers: a log line, no card; an outpost whose defence is 1.5 × a band's strength (`RAID_HOLD`, 3.5 a raider) sees the band size it up and move on (back to the pool), no card.
- **The kills** (settleWave): the grounds' share first (campscout takeDead), then the drawn are put back (putBack, deadpop putBackRaiders: new, #69) and the killed taken off through killDead / killRaiders (the kill memory and the noise; loud when guns were used). A wave whose card was dropped (a save made with the card open) goes back the next hour (settleStale).
- **The AI** (ADR 0001): the groups' homes (main bases and outposts, their camps; `h.grounds` when #70 gives a POI camp grounds) roll by the same rules on the same pools; a wave is fought by the numbers at once: their defence (2 a person at home + 3 a wall works) against the wave (a raider counts 2): all killed when it holds, else as many as it holds, the rest put back, the food × 0.95. The governor answers the cards with Defend (the default).
- **The Camp tab** (Overview's Status, every base): "Nearby: the dead medium, raiders low · an attack every ~30 h" (campattack nearbyView; ui/ui nearbyRow), the hover with the dead's and the raiders' rates and the multipliers now. The levels are the player's estimate (threatknow tileThreatEstimate, #67: live while the base is on the tile), the rates from the pools. The 7-day forecast (forecast campAttacks, beside #67's deadNear) lists the expected attacks on their days, Watch, "possible".
- **Saves**: `base.campAttack = { after, rafter, n, wave }` (the next hour one may come, the next hour a band may come, the attacks so far, the wave out now) is plain data; an older save has none and starts with no cooldown.
- **Numbers** (`tools/campattacktest.js`, seed 42, a rural camp, 60 days at 0 grounds): 15% of the cap 0 attacks, 75% 35 (about every 41 h, day and night), 75% and loud (noise 30) 85, empty 0; raiders at their cap 9 bands, at 15% 0; two camps on one tile 40 between them against one camp's 35 (the second gets the tile's roll only while the first is busy or cooling down); an AI home on a high tile 14 in 60 days, its kills taking the open ground from 80 to 14.
- Modules: [campattack, campscout, deadpop, staging, forecast, ui/ui]; tests `tools/campattacktest.js`, `tools/campscouttest.js`.

### Rooms and the way out
The user's request 2026-10-26 (the heat epic #64 §4 "Rooms are Cleared or Not cleared" and "Cut off", issue #71; the rules confirmed the same day, climbing out and arrivals fighting in among them; build mode's "cleared" tightened by #64's addendum):
- "Add the fog rule, cleared and not cleared rooms for use in defend zombie wave events and for when you actively go back into the map to clear the zombies."
- "One thing else that needs to be handled is getting encircled. The survivors cannot leave if they are encircled with the camp. Let's say we are in a building and we are on the second floor but we are running from zombies in the first floor and set up camp in the second floor how do we handle this? As the base allows them to leave but in the raid map they would not be able to how do we determine if they are able to leave so we can block some base actions like gathering etc as they can't leave the raid."

All of it is [poirooms] (tunables `POIROOMS`), with hooks in [poicamp], [campattack], [campscout], [fort], [parties]; test `tools/poiroomstest.js` (71 checks).

**Zones** (zonesOf): every room of the POI's building on every floor the record keeps (`P.rm`; a record from before #71 only the camp floor's `P.rooms`), and the yard's open areas: the ground floor's cells outside the rooms in blocks of 8 × 8 (`YARD_BLOCK`), named "the yard (north-west)" and so on. A cell inside a room (off its walls) is that room's; a room's wall line (its doors and gaps) and an upper floor's cells outside every room are thresholds (passable when seen).

**Cleared or Not cleared** (roomState; no threat number):
- **Cleared**: seen (60% of its floor seen by your side, `SEEN_SHARE`, or emptied in a fight by the numbers: `P.cz`) and none of the dead in it (`P.zd`).
- **Not cleared**: fogged, or the dead seen or known in it.
- **Builds** go only in Cleared rooms and on seen, empty yard: poiPlaceWhy asks cellClearWhy ("Not cleared: the kitchen (dead known)" / "(fogged)"), on the base map and in build mode on the raid map alike (build mode's "cleared" is this rule now). The Build list's "No room on this map" counts it (poiFits). A build site in a room that turns Not cleared stops ("Stopped: … is not cleared (its progress is kept)", buildWorkProblem); a queued build there waits (buildings constructionHour → poicamp siteRoomWhy), its progress kept.
- **The base map** tints the Not cleared rooms and the yard with the dead as no-build ground (markNotCleared: `c.nb`, the hover says why).

**The dead per room** (syncRooms): the POI's surviving dead (deadpop poiDead) live in its Not cleared rooms and fogged yard, kept per zone in `P.zd`, and always sum to the pool: whenever they are read, the rooms are put right against it.
- More in the pool than in the rooms (the daily refill, a wave that broke in, the tile's trickle): into the Not cleared zones nearest the map's edge first, each up to one dead a 6 cells of its floor (`HOLD_CELLS`, at least `HOLD_MIN` 2) before the next, then one each round; never into the camp's own room while any other will do. Rooms turn Not cleared again this way.
- Fewer (kills by the numbers, another group's raid on the place): off the zones holding the most.
- Kills in a fight come off the very rooms they came out of (roomsKilled). Every room Cleared means the pool is 0.
- The camp done (foundPoiCamp → deadFromMap): the dead on the map go into the rooms they stand in, tagged (`u.room`), so a kill later in that raid comes off its room; the rest of the pool by the edge order.
- A POI camp's grounds (campscout `base.grounds`) are the same count: syncRooms keeps `base.grounds.dead` = the pool (and makes the grounds again when the dead come back).

**Defend waves out of the rooms** (campattack waveSource → roomWave): the grounds' part of a wave comes out of the Not cleared rooms by their route to the camp (a cost search over the floors joined by their stairs: a step 1, a shut door 8 more, `DOOR`). Open routes first; a room behind a shut or barricaded door (a closed, barred or locked door, boards, a barricade) lets out at most half its dead in one wave (`DOOR_SLOW`, at least 1). The wave records them (`w.rooms`). On the camp's map (defendWave → fort fortDefence → placeFromRooms, the battle's own dice) those dead start in their rooms; a room on another floor than the camp's sends them up or down the stairs; the tile's trickle comes from the edges as before. settleWave takes the kills off their rooms; a fight lost (`brokeIn`) lets the trickle's survivors into the place (into the POI's pool: the Not cleared rooms nearest the edge). The way out is worked out again after every attack.

**Going back in** (the clearing fight): any raid on the camp's POI map opens it with the rooms as they stand (poiCampRaidStart → roomsOnRaid): the POI's dead on the map put in their Not cleared rooms (`u.room`), what was seen of every floor known again. The team clears room by room and can leave at any time: at the end (poiCampAfterRaid → roomsAfterRaid) the dead killed come off their rooms, what was seen is kept for every floor, and the way out is worked out again. "Clear the dead from the base grounds" on a POI camp is this raid in the browser (campscout groundsRaid → scavenge startScavenge on the POI); headless it stays the fight by the numbers, its kills off the rooms on the blocked route first (roomsCleared), a room emptied that way counting as seen.

**The way out** (wayOut, cached in `P.way = { open, climb, through, why, h }`; computeWay):
- **Open**: a walkable route from the camp's 5 × 5 to an exit through Cleared rooms, seen thresholds and seen, empty yard only (the camp's own room is always theirs). A breadth-first search over the floors joined by their stairs (the same x, y, stairs on both floors). Exits: any ground-floor edge cell, or a ground-floor door or gap of a room's wall onto the yard. A record from before #71 on an upper floor (the floor below never kept) counts its stairs as exits.
- **Climb**: no route, but a window (a window or a broken one) next to a reached cell over seen, empty ground on the ground floor: a rope or a siege ladder (the camp's armory or on someone home) or knotted sheets (a bed at the camp) for an upper floor's window; a ground-floor window is vaulted. It counts for people on foot, **not for furniture, heavy loads or vehicles** (`climb`: cutOffWhy with `heavy`).
- **Cut off**: neither. `through` names the Not cleared zone on the nearest walkable route ("Cut off: the way out runs through the hall (dead known)", or "(fogged)"; "no walkable way out of the building" when there is none at all).
- **When**: the camp done, after every grounds attack (settleWave), after every clearing fight (poiCampAfterRaid, Break out, a fight in, the grounds cleared by the numbers), and after the daily recalculation (world tickHour → poiRoomsDaily, after deadpop's day and spread); never every hour: everything else reads the cache. A change says so in the log and on a card ("Way out: cut off" / "Way out: open").

**Cut off** (cutOffWhy): the camp still stands; its people rest, eat, craft, cook, build and place fittings in Cleared rooms, barricade and fight the waves. **Blocked**, greyed with the reason:
- the land jobs off the map: Cut wood, Forage, Hunt (snares too), Fish, Gather stone, Fetch water (their woodWorkWhy, forageWorkWhy, huntWorkWhy, fishWorkWhy, stoneWorkWhy, fetchWaterWhy: the activity line says "Can't forage: Cut off: …", and the governor does not schedule them), and Scavenge (activity tripWhy);
- hauling (hauling haulActivity, `heavy`: a climb does not do);
- every team leaving (parties launchExpedition: a team, a supply run, Hunt the dead, a field work's crew, the governor's trips; a vehicle needs a real route: `heavy`).
- Felling marked on the camp's own map is not leaving it and goes on.

**Arrivals fight their way in** (parties returnHome → fightIn): a team of yours coming home to a cut-off camp fights in. In the browser a raid on the POI map from the edge (the camp stands on it; Settle in once safe moves them in; leaving by an edge leaves them a team on the tile). Headless a fight by the numbers against up to 20 (`FIGHT_MAX`) of the dead on the blocked route (handler "poiFightIn"): won, its kills off those rooms and home; lost, the team waits for orders outside. No haul route runs into a cut-off camp (hauling routeWork: a load cannot get in).

**Break out** (breakOut, the Camp tab's button beside "Way out: cut off"; handler "poiBreakOut"): a fight on the camp's map (the base defence layout, the defenders placed at the camp) with whoever is home against up to 20 of the dead in the rooms on the blocked route, placed in their rooms; the kills come off those rooms and the way is worked out again. breakOutProblem says why not (the way is open, nobody home, a fight on, no dead to fight: the route is only fogged).

**UI**: the Camp tab ("Around the camp") and the base's Overview card (Status) show **Way out: open** / **open (a climb: on foot only)** / **cut off: …**, the rooms' states in the hover, and Break out when cut off (ui wayRow; main `breakout`). The base map tints the Not cleared rooms.

**Headless, the governor and the AI** (docs/adr/0001): the same checks everywhere; the governor (autopilot autopilotHour, 09:00 daily) breaks out of a cut-off camp with two or more people home (govBreakOut), and its jobs and teams are blocked by the same functions. The AI groups set up no camps inside POIs (#70 gave them none), so none of theirs is ever cut off; any base with a `poiCamp` record is read by the same rules.

**Saves**: `P.zd`, `P.cz`, `P.way`, `P.rm`, `P.fl`, `P.seenF` are plain data on base.poiCamp; a wave's `rooms` on base.campAttack.wave. A record from before #71 (`v` 1, the camp's floor only) loads as it was: its rooms are seeded from the pool on first read by the edge order (deterministic), its way out worked out then, and the next raid on its map adds its other floors. No dice outside battles (orders by distance and id).

**Decisions made in #71** (recorded for the user): a room is "seen" at 60% of its floor; the yard's open areas are 8 × 8 blocks; a room holds one of the dead a 6 cells before the next takes them; a shut door lets out half a room's dead a wave; knotted sheets are any bed at the camp; a ground-floor window counts as a climb without a kit; the camp's own room is always passable for its people; felling on the camp's own map is not blocked; Break out and a fight in meet at most 20 of the dead a fight.
