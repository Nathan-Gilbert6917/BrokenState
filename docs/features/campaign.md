## Normal and Hardcore: the road start
Issue #79. Normal and Hardcore both open the day Calder falls, on the road; Hardcore adds the hard rules below.
- The user's requests 2026-10-27: "I want to change how normal mode is to basically be what hardcore mode is right now but having a default 4 starting survivors and one vehicle but you can have up to 8 starting survivors"; "Start like in hardcore with no hub but having tents and need to build a camp to start a base"; "Old saves do not matter"; "Do the code split".
- **The split** [roadstart isRoadStart isHardcore roadScreenMode]: `isRoadStart` is the opening (no hub, the outbreak year, the bands, the half-looted sites, the opening cards, Pick start's open land: everything the road-start section below describes, which used to be `isHardcore`); `isHardcore` is the hard rules only. `state.start` is `"road"` or `"hub"` (createWorld's `roadStart`; save.js gives a save without it its mode's: Hardcore road, else hub). `state.hardcore` keeps its old name for the road start's data; the fleet kind and the points bill still call a road start `"hardcore"` (startfleet `fleetKind`, custompoints).
- **Who opens where**: Normal and Hardcore on the road (main.js `runOpts` passes `roadStart: roadScreenMode(ui.mode)`); Custom and Crashlanding at a hub; a headless tool's createWorld with no `roadStart` keeps the old default (Hardcore road, anything else the hub), so the tests' hub worlds are as they were.
- **Starting people** [hardcore NORMAL_PEOPLE START_PEOPLE HUB_PEOPLE peopleRange]: Normal 1–8, four by default; Hardcore 1–4, three; the hub start (Custom's own count aside, the tools' default) 1–8, six. A road start of five to eight: one car, the rest of the fleet as set (Starting vehicles: up to one a survivor), a tent bag each (tents hardcoreTents).
- Achievements: a row's `"road"` mode is any road start (A roof at last, A real hub, Life on the road); `runMode` is the screen's mode (hardcore only for Hardcore).
- Test: `tools/modesplittest.js`.

## Hardcore's hard rules
The user's requests 2026-10-27: "I want hardcore mode to actually be hardcore"; "do all the suggested hardcore changes except injuries are either permanent or not permanent just take longer to heal". Every number is in `hardrules HARD`; nothing here touches Normal (`hardMult`, `hardDial` are 1 outside Hardcore).
- **Stakes**: always Ironman (ui/newgame `takeModeSet` ticks and locks the box, puts it back on leaving; main.js `ironman` and `gamemode`: the crew back as rolled), so no Customize points; no Keep playing after an ending (ui/ui `keepPlayingBtn`, main.js `keepplaying`).
- **Scarcity and wear** (worldcfg `cfgOf` × `hardDial`, on top of the run's World… dials): hunger and thirst ×1.2, food spoilage ×1.5, sickness ×1.3, durability loss and failures ×1.5, fuel use ×1.25; loot food and water ×0.75, ammo and meds ×0.5, guns ×0.8, fuel ×0.6, parts ×0.8, scrap ×0.9; the sites start at `LOOT_START` 0.8 of a normal start's loot instead of twice it (hardcore-loot `startLoot`), the raid map's richness cap 1.2 (combat createRaid); the car flees with `START_FUEL` 0.35 of the dice's tank (hardcore-start rollCar).
- **Injuries**: nothing permanent; every heal at `HEAL` 0.6 of the usual speed (medical `healRatePerHour`, orders `campHealPerHour`, an infected wound clearing on its own), an infected wound `INFECT` 1.4 times worse (its drain at a base and in the field, the chance it sets in).
- **The threat**: the dead's damage ×1.15, bites ×1.5 and the land ×1.25 for the worm, raids ×1.3, the dead at sites ×1.15, hive growth ×1.25; the director (`directorMult`): hordes, raids, hive growth and the dead at sites × 1 + 0.04 a survivor over three + 0.05 a base, up to ×1.8, cached for the hour; Field Heat ×2 (fieldheat `hardNoise`); the odds of an attack on a camp or base ×1.5 (campattack `attackMult`); raiders from the first days (hardcore-groups `hcNoRaiders`); a base overrun loses half again of its stores (factions, `OVERRUN_TAKE`).
- **People**: tiredness ×1.25 (sleep), grief ×1.5 (relations), a mental break's mornings counted under mood 16, not 10 (mentalbreaks `snapAt`), half the strangers on a recruit card and half the walkers' chance (recruits), the bands' goodwill −15 at the start and every group's −0.1 a day down to −40 (hardcore `hardGoodwillDaily`).
- **Information** (`fogged`): no threat estimates (threatknow `tileThreatEstimate`, `poiDeadEstimate`, `threatOverlay`), no forecast meter (ui/threatbar), "today" or "a while ago" for the map's last-seen times (ui/render `ageText`), no card answers itself when its time runs out (ui/pacing `expire`; the tray's ✓ all still does).
- **Reward**: Hardcore's own achievements (Hardcore: a year and a day, Hardcore: a village) and a ☠ on its endings in the Hall of fame (ui/halloffame).
- Test: `tools/modesplittest.js`.

## Bare start and the hub's start layout
Bases start bare; the hub start (Custom since issue #79; Normal before it) starts with a fixed layout.
- Every base starts with empty plots [world HUB_START].
- The user's requests 2026-10-22: "the normal mode base map should start with 6 beds in a building or some tents and beds in a building". The normal hub starts with a Lodge with four beds set in it, a two-person tent pitched from its bag and eight crop plots dug (sown before the fall), placed by the game [world HUB_LAYOUT / hubLayout].
- Where: out in the open (never a town: startpick isSettlementTile), START_CLEAR (5) tiles or more from towns, cities, hives and the Guard's bases, on a road where one fits, named for the nearest town [world createWorld, startpick startNear].

## New-run shortcuts
Three ways to start a run quickly: roll every setting at once, move the start on the same map, or click the start on the map.

- *Random run* rolls every setting (seed, size, groups, hives, mode, starting people, zombie look, the crew and gear, Customize's points) and starts at once [ui/newgame rollRandomRun].
- *New start position* moves where you start on the same map and seed, crew kept.
- *Pick start on map* (the user's request 2026-10-22) opens the map; the tile you click is the start.
  - A hub start (Custom; Normal until issue #79) (the user's request 2026-10-22): open land for the hub (not water, a mountain, a town or a city), at least START_CLEAR = 5 tiles from towns, cities, hives, the Guard's bases and other groups' bases.
  - A road start (Normal and Hardcore since issue #79): any open land that is not a hive or a group's or the Guard's base.
  - The hover tip says why a tile cannot be one ("Too close to the town of …").
- Modules: [ui/newgame rollRandomRun, startpick startWhyNot startNear START_CLEAR chosenStart, main pickStartAt]; test `tools/startpicktest.js`.

The date played is passed in, never read from the clock in the sim.
- An entry (HALL.BYTES 2 KB at most; `fit` drops the notables' reasons, then notables, then the founders' details): the run (campaign, date, mode by achievements runMode, world size, seed, Custom's counts, Ironman); the ending (kind, label, how for a defeat: everyone died or some gone, every base lost, the last death; the day, season and year); days, peak people (history's pop column and now), people at the end, deaths (the memorial wall), bases at the end and at most at once (history's base rows), hives destroyed, groups in the coalition; the founders (achievements foundersOf) and their fates (alive, captive, left, died day N with the cause); up to 8 notable survivors, each for one thing (longest with the group, the best skill, the most of the dead put down, people killed, built, treated, trips, hives) with name, background, top skill and fate; the leader at the end; the run's achievements as [id, day]. Fields an older world lacks are left out.
- Recording [ui/halloffame]: `hallRecord` on each redraw (main.js refresh) while the run is over: the entry added once by its key, so a save reloaded at its end screen adds nothing and a run kept playing that reaches another ending (or falls) gets a second one. The end screens say "Added to the Hall of fame" (`hallEndHTML`) with a button to it.
- Storage: browser storage key `brokenstate.halloffame` (localStorage, as the save list's index in ui/saves; the desktop app's page keeps it under its fixed origin), every access in try/catch (no storage, one that throws, rubbish in the key: an empty list, nothing written; full: the older half dropped and tried once more). Nothing leaves the device but Export's download.
- The window: 🏛 Hall of fame on the title screen (ui/title) and from the end screens; the list newest first with filters by ending and mode; an entry's detail with Export (the entry as JSON, ui/saves downloadText) and Delete (asks first). Esc closes it.
- Test `tools/halloffametest.js`: entries for the coalition's and the hive victory, a defeat (from tools/winforce.js's forced start) and Crashlanding's bunker, their fields and size; building one changes nothing (the dice, the save); a second ending after Keep playing has its own key; no duplicate key; the 200 limit; filters; storage through a stand-in, a throwing one, a full one, none and rubbish; the window; an older world.
