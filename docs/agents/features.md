# Broken State: major features

A map of the game's major features, for agents getting oriented. Built from the header comment of every module in `prototype/src/sim` and `prototype/src/ui`, plus `prototype/README.md` (2026-10-13). The modules for each feature are in brackets: `[name]` means `prototype/src/sim/name.js`, and `[ui/name]` means `prototype/src/ui/name.js`. When you add or remove a major system, update this file too.

## 1. Campaign, world and pacing
- **Procedural State of Calder**: hex map with terrain, rivers, cities, towns, countryside sites, roads and infection [mapgen, hex, worldsize]. World sizes run Small, Normal, Large and Huge, each with its own AI group count.
- **Hourly and daily simulation tick**, seeded and deterministic [world, rng].
- **Seasons and weather**: four 28-day seasons, regional weather fronts, frozen rivers in winter that people and the dead can cross [weather, rivers].
- **Eras**: early game (years 1–4), mid game (5–9) and late game (10+), with threats that grow each year [eras].
- **Two victories**: destroy every hive, or win with the Calder Coalition (an alliance holding over half the land). "Keep playing" is available after either one [world, alliance].
- **Hardcore mode**: start on the day Calder falls with 1–4 survivors on the road, no hub, sites only half looted, and the outbreak year [hardcore*, hardcore-start, hardcore-loot, hardcore-outbreak, hardcore-groups]. The outbreak year has road and hub cards of its own (gridlock, Guard checkpoints and relief drops, the emergency broadcast, holdouts, looters, the newly dead; hungry people at the gate, a Guard deserter, the radio going quiet, city refugees) [events HC_EVENTS, hubevents hc_*].
- **Bare start**: every base starts with empty plots [world HUB_START].
- **New-run shortcuts**: *Random run* rolls every setting (seed, size, groups, hives, mode, starting people, zombie look, the crew and gear, Customize's points) and starts at once; *New start position* moves where you start on the same map and seed, crew kept [ui/newgame rollRandomRun, startpick].
- **World configuration**: the new-run screen's *World…* window sets a run's dials (hunger, thirst, spoilage, sickness; horde size, the dead at sites, hive growth, zombie damage, infection from bites and from the land, whether the worm is curable; the AI groups' starting gear, events, raids; loot by kind; the sizes of bases, city crawls and hive crawls; wear, failures, fuel), with presets (Gentle, Standard, Harsh, Nightmare) that turn to Custom once a dial moves. Kept on the world (`state.worldCfg`, saved) and read where each rate is applied [worldcfg, ui/worldcfg].
- **Outbreak beyond the border**: keeps a minimum number of hives alive [outbreak].
- **Event cards and pacing**: pause rules, a card tray, auto-answers, and speeds from 1× to 5× [cardrules, ui/pacing].
- **Hub events**: home decisions every few days (a fire, a quarrel, a peddler, rats in the stores, the dead at the fence, a sick traveller, a cold snap, music at night, a wreck to strip and more) [hubevents]. Also the harvest festival [festival].
- **7-day threat forecast** meter [forecast].

- **Skills, XP, teaching, books and perks** [skills]. **Traits** with tiers and points [traits].

- **Sleep and tiredness** [sleep].
- **Morale**: crowding, leader lift, desertion warnings and desertion [morale]. **Mental breaks** [mentalbreaks]. **Comforts and treats** [comforts].

- **Family**: couples, pregnancy, births and children growing up [family].

- **Appearance and sprites**, plus survivor customization (backgrounds, customization points, starting gear, random builds) [appearance, custombg, custompoints, startgear, randomkit]. A random kit with a gun in the hands (Build, Randomize all, the gear window's dice under Customize) carries its spare magazines and a box or two of its common round in a pocket, a pouch or the pack, a box's points kept back while the other slots are drawn (the user's request 2026-10-18) [randomkit randomKit putRounds / putBox]. Every body on the battle map is a low-poly figure that faces its way and turns with the view: survivors, human enemies, the dead in every kind (the plain, the special, the armoured, the risen), the bosses, and the fallen lying along the ground; the panels show the same figures and the inventory spins one through 360°, which the player can turn by hand instead (a drag or the wheel over it stops it where it is left, a degree a pixel; ↻ Spin or a double-click sets it turning again; per figure by its spin key, so a rebuilt panel keeps the turn) [ui/figures figureSpinImg, handOf, holdFigure, dragFigure, wheelFigure, turnFigure, spinFigure, spinFrame, frameOfYaw; ui/sprites, ui/hivesprites and ui/downsprites keep the flat painters as fallbacks]. On the State Map every moving piece (your teams on foot and by car, boat or raft, the groups' parties and convoys, bands and nomads, outsiders, hordes, herds, workers on job trips) is a low-poly figure too, built from the same limb at the map's scale, facing the way it travels (every 15°, no mirroring) and animated while on its way (walk cycles, a limp, wheels turning, oxen trotting, oars pulling, the dead shuffling) [ui/mapfigures; ui/render cycleFrame and headingOf, ui/workermap; the places keep ui/mapsprites' flat painters].
- **Status icons** and a "Why did this happen?" cause chain for deaths and lost bases [status, causes].

## 3. Bases and economy
- **Base tiers**: camp, shelter, outpost and hub. Camps can be set up, struck and upgraded [staging].
- **Buildings and construction queue**: plots by base size, demolition refunds, water barrels and storage crates [buildings]. Field works outside the walls [fieldworks].
- **Base storage limits** (the user's request 2026-10-19): a base's storage is kg of room (the core by tier: a camp 0, a shelter 150, an outpost 500, the main hub 1200; storage buildings: storage box 60, crates 150, root cellar 400, cold store and freezer 300, storage shed 600, storehouse 2000, warehouse 6000 at an outpost or the hub; a car parked there its payload while it is parked; an opened lock box in the armory BOX_STORE_KG); the stock and the armory fill it by worth a kg and what does not fit lies outside, where items wear every day (twice in rain or snow) and raiders find it easier: on the raid map what lies outside is piles of crates in the yard (raiders go for them first and grab twice as much a turn) and a parked car that is storage is a container; a parked car can be moved to any free cells it fits (Move: the base map with it picked). The fire safe and the rifle case are carried in both arms only (twoHands: no pocket, pack or pouch; both hands full) [basestorage storageOf / storageSummary / storageLine / outsideShare / storageDaily, buildings STORE_KG, data-boxes BOX_STORE_KG, inventory isTwoHands, carry handsCount, basemap layPiles / makeStores / thiefTurn / carLen, ui/basemap moveCarButton; `tools/basestoragetest.js`, `tools/basestoragemaptest.js`]. The base window shows it (the Overview's Storage row, the camp's Stores card, storage kg on the Construction tab, a parked car's storage and its Move button on the Vehicles tab, the Armory's Storage card with "Outside: weathering" and "Both hands" badges) [ui/ui, ui/armoryview, ui/itemlabels]; the autopilot queues the smallest storage that holds what lies outside, after its other priorities [autopilot storageWish]. The AI groups build storage by the same rules (ADR 0001): when goods lie outside one of their bases (measured as the supply run leaves the stores tonight; a camp of theirs has no core room, as yours), they put up your governor's choice from its ladder (a storage box at an outpost camp, crates or a shed at an outpost, crates, a shed, the storehouse or a warehouse at the main base), the smallest that holds it, else the biggest they can pay for, at your costs, your max counts, the warehouse's Mechanics 2 and size rule, your hours as crew-days, after water, fields, quarters and walls under threat; the buildings stand at their bases (what raiders find inside or on the piles) and the Groups card names them [groupworks storageWork / storeLock / storeDef / groupStorage, groupbases WORKS_BUILDING / stockTonight / worksBuildings; `tools/groupstoragetest.js`]. Storming a group's base puts that base's real stock and spare kit on the assault map in their storage (piles for what does not fit, a locked armory to force); your people take from it through the raid search window and what they carry out leaves the group [assaultstores, fort, conquest, raidloot; `tools/assaultstoretest.js`, `tools/groupstoragetest.js`].
- **The base map**: every base's own grid map (a camp 40×40 up to a hub 300×300) with the camp, shelter or base in the middle; buildings, crop plots, defences and parked cars placed by hand (anywhere on the map, right against anything, only never on top of something else, on the wall's band or in a doorway) or by the game; outlines for buildings going up; the map a horde or a raid fights over [basemap, ui/basemap].
- **Work priorities** (RimWorld-style work tab): roles, a 1–5 priority grid and an hourly scheduler [work]. Also idle-worker assignment [idlework], the watch roster [watch], base stewards and leaders run by the governor [steward], and bulk roster actions [bulk].
- **Daily economy and upkeep per head** [economy, upkeep].
- **Water**: purified vs un-purified, wells and filters [water, claims].
- **Food**: food split by type with its own shelf life, spoiling, storage, preserving and jarring [foods, storage]. Also mealtimes [meals], the food chain and food ledger views [foodchain, foodlog].
- **Cooking**: Rimworld-style recipes from ingredients, food poisoning and the kitchen [cooking, kitchen].
- **Farming** with crops, plots, seasons and greenhouses [farming].
- **Hunting, snares, fishing and foraging** [hunting, foraging].
- **Woodcutting and heating**: firewood first, then fuel; every fire burns it (a camp's or shelter's fire by the hour for its people's warmth, boiling and the pot; the night's fire at every base on a cool night, the whole day's heating in the frost; the boiler and the Kitchen's stove) [woodcutting, upkeep, needs].
- **Job trips**: outdoor workers walk out to the base's land, with events for a lone worker and gear handed out for those trips [jobtrips, tripland, tripevents, tripgear].
- **Crafting**: hand crafting plus standing production orders/bills [crafting, production]. A recipe can make a stack (`CRAFTS[k].n`, makeshift picks: three a craft) that joins one of the same thing in the armory or on the team [crafting `craftItem`, `craftingHour`, `fieldCraft`; production `craftOrderHave` counts the stack; itemstack `mergeInto`].
- **Power**: generators and an ethanol still [power].
- **Breakdowns**: fixtures wear out and need a named survivor to repair them [breakdowns].
- **Blueprints and manuals** that unlock top tiers [blueprints].
- **Supply runs** between your own bases [supply]. **Moving people** between bases and the overcrowding card [moveout].
- **Hidden bases**: run silent, camouflage [hiding].

## 6. Tactical combat (XCOM-style)

Third-partying: a bottle or firecrackers you throw next to their group pull the unaware dead there, and the dead fall on them.
