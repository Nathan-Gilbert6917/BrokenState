# Broken State: major features

A map of the game's major features, for agents getting oriented. Built from the header comment of every module in `prototype/src/sim` and `prototype/src/ui`, plus `prototype/README.md` (2026-10-13). The modules for each feature are in brackets: `[name]` means `prototype/src/sim/name.js`, and `[ui/name]` means `prototype/src/ui/name.js`. When you add or remove a major system, update this file too.

## 1. Campaign, world and pacing
- **Procedural State of Calder**: hex map with terrain, rivers, cities, towns, countryside sites, roads and infection [mapgen, hex, worldsize]. World sizes run Small, Normal, Large and Huge, each with its own AI group count.
- **Hourly and daily simulation tick**, seeded and deterministic [world, rng].
- **Seasons and weather**: four 28-day seasons, regional weather fronts, frozen rivers in winter that people and the dead can cross [weather, rivers].
- **Eras**: early game (years 1–4), mid game (5–9) and late game (10+), with threats that grow each year [eras].
- **Two victories**: destroy every hive, or win with the Calder Coalition (an alliance holding over half the land). "Keep playing" is available after either one [world, alliance].
- **Hardcore mode**: start on the day Calder falls with 1–4 survivors on the road, no hub, sites only half looted, and the outbreak year [hardcore*, hardcore-start, hardcore-loot, hardcore-outbreak, hardcore-groups].
- **Bare start**: every base starts with empty plots [world HUB_START].
- **New-run shortcuts**: *Random run* rolls every setting (seed, size, groups, hives, mode, starting people, zombie look, the crew and gear, Customize's points) and starts at once; *New start position* moves where you start on the same map and seed, crew kept [ui/newgame rollRandomRun, startpick].
- **Outbreak beyond the border**: keeps a minimum number of hives alive [outbreak].
- **Event cards and pacing**: pause rules, a card tray, auto-answers, and speeds from 1× to 5× [cardrules, ui/pacing].
- **Hub events**: home decisions every few days [hubevents]. Also the harvest festival [festival].
- **7-day threat forecast** meter [forecast].

## 2. Survivors
- **Skills, XP, teaching, books and perks** [skills]. **Traits** with tiers and points [traits].
- **Needs**: hunger, thirst and mood gauges; eating person by person with a meal mood ladder, food preferences and ration levels [needs, eating].
- **Sleep and tiredness** [sleep].
- **Morale**: crowding, leader lift, desertion warnings and desertion [morale]. **Mental breaks** [mentalbreaks]. **Comforts and treats** [comforts].
- **Relationships**: grief, service record, memorial wall and daily mood factors [relations].
- **Family**: couples, pregnancy, births and children growing up [family].
- **Recruits**: newcomer choice cards for strangers, refugees, loners and returning deserters [recruits].
- **Appearance and sprites**, plus survivor customization (backgrounds, customization points, starting gear, random builds) [appearance, custombg, custompoints, startgear, randomkit]. Every body on the battle map is a low-poly figure that faces its way and turns with the view: survivors, human enemies, the dead in every kind (the plain, the special, the armoured, the risen), the bosses, and the fallen lying along the ground; the panels show the same figures and the inventory spins one through 360°, which the player can turn by hand instead (a drag or the wheel over it stops it where it is left, a degree a pixel; ↻ Spin or a double-click sets it turning again; per figure by its spin key, so a rebuilt panel keeps the turn) [ui/figures figureSpinImg, handOf, holdFigure, dragFigure, wheelFigure, turnFigure, spinFigure, spinFrame, frameOfYaw; ui/sprites, ui/hivesprites and ui/downsprites keep the flat painters as fallbacks]. On the State Map every moving piece (your teams on foot and by car, boat or raft, the groups' parties and convoys, bands and nomads, outsiders, hordes, herds, workers on job trips) is a low-poly figure too, built from the same limb at the map's scale, facing the way it travels (every 15°, no mirroring) and animated while on its way (walk cycles, a limp, wheels turning, oxen trotting, oars pulling, the dead shuffling) [ui/mapfigures; ui/render cycleFrame and headingOf, ui/workermap; the places keep ui/mapsprites' flat painters].
- **Status icons** and a "Why did this happen?" cause chain for deaths and lost bases [status, causes].

## 3. Bases and economy
- **Base tiers**: camp, shelter, outpost and hub. Camps can be set up, struck and upgraded [staging].
- **Buildings and construction queue**: plots by base size, demolition refunds, water barrels and storage crates [buildings]. Field works outside the walls [fieldworks].
- **Work priorities** (RimWorld-style work tab): roles, a 1–5 priority grid and an hourly scheduler [work]. Also idle-worker assignment [idlework], the watch roster [watch], base stewards and leaders run by the governor [steward], and bulk roster actions [bulk].
- **Daily economy and upkeep per head** [economy, upkeep].
- **Water**: purified vs un-purified, wells and filters [water, claims].
- **Food**: food split by type with its own shelf life, spoiling, storage, preserving and jarring [foods, storage]. Also mealtimes [meals], the food chain and food ledger views [foodchain, foodlog].
- **Cooking**: Rimworld-style recipes from ingredients, food poisoning and the kitchen [cooking, kitchen].
- **Farming** with crops, plots, seasons and greenhouses [farming].
- **Hunting, snares, fishing and foraging** [hunting, foraging].
- **Woodcutting and heating**: firewood first, then fuel [woodcutting].
- **Job trips**: outdoor workers walk out to the base's land, with events for a lone worker and gear handed out for those trips [jobtrips, tripland, tripevents, tripgear].
- **Crafting**: hand crafting plus standing production orders/bills [crafting, production]. A recipe can make a stack (`CRAFTS[k].n`, makeshift picks: three a craft) that joins one of the same thing in the armory or on the team [crafting `craftItem`, `craftingHour`, `fieldCraft`; production `craftOrderHave` counts the stack; itemstack `mergeInto`].
- **Power**: generators and an ethanol still [power].
- **Breakdowns**: fixtures wear out and need a named survivor to repair them [breakdowns].
- **Blueprints and manuals** that unlock top tiers [blueprints].
- **Supply runs** between your own bases [supply]. **Moving people** between bases and the overcrowding card [moveout].
- **Hidden bases**: run silent, camouflage [hiding].

- **Discovery and hidden sites**, the Sweep area mission (the old radius Survey), rumours, and map markers with last-seen times [discovery, survey, markers].
- **Points of interest (POIs)**: 0–4 small places per land tile (house, shed, hunting blind, gas station…) with their own loot pools, a town suburb ring, known/hidden by zone, finds carried in team reports to the hub, and saves that hold only the changes [pois, mapgen; the kinds in data LOCATIONS]. On the map: a sprite per kind (ui/mapsprites POI_PAINT), a bare tile's top POI drawn on its zone plate once known and faded by its pool, a pip with the number of places known on the tile, parties sharing a tile drawn on opposite sides of the hex [ui/render]; the tile panel's Places rows, the hover text and the codex's Places category (never a count of what is hidden) [ui/poiview, ui/ui renderRight, ui/codex]. ## 6. Tactical combat (XCOM-style)
- **Turn-based tactical battles** with an isometric view [combat, ui/isoview].
- **Lost pictures drawn again**: every kept picture (sprites, figures, the battle's ground, fog, edge, light masks and marks, the map's chunks and shade, the minimap, the clouds, the gun art) is dropped and drawn again when the browser's GPU context is lost and restored (the game's canvases report `contextlost` / `contextrestored`; a sentinel picture is read back once a second from the frame loop for a loss the page is not told about), so a battle no longer goes on with ringed units and no bodies after a driver reset [ui/pictures; onForgetPictures in each module; ui/combat initCombatUI, ui/render initRenderer; desktop/main.js logs a GPU process gone].
- **Battle map sizes** by context, from 24×18 up to 48×34, with anti-slog endings [battlesize].
- **A raid without the side panel** (the user's request 2026-10-18): a raid on a site (`b.raid`) is played with the whole screen as the map. What the panel carried is a strip along the top (the place, the turn and the clock, Heat with its bar, the floor shown with ▲ ▼ in a building with storeys, what is carried, the ammo and meds, what is left to search, the ways out, the keys, the night; at the right ⚙ Menu, the view's buttons and 📜 Log), cards over the map (the choices before you go in, the building to search, a warning, the hover details, the result) and the log in its own small window (shown or hidden by the Log button or `bview.log`, folded to its title bar, dragged by that bar, resized by its corner; where it is kept per machine). Every other battle keeps the side panel [ui/combat `raidLayout`, `renderRaidTop`, `renderRaidCards`, `renderRaidLog`, `renderRaidLogFoot`; ui/logwindow; main.js renderCombat; styles.css `#combat.noside`, `#cbtop`, `#cbcards`, `#cblogwin`; `tools/raidscreentest.js`].
- **Before you go in**: a raid (an assault, a rescue) opens with the plan as a window over the battle screen, the site under the fog of war and nobody of yours placed until you go in: send a scout first (the odds), wait for night, Look at the map first (only once a scout came back unseen: the window put aside, a chip in the strip brings it back), the team's kit (Team inventory, Teams together; the battle made again with the kit as it stands), go in from a side (North / East / South / West: that places the team and starts the fight; one way in: Go in), or Leave (the raid called off, nobody hurt) [raidplan, ui/raidplan].
- **Raids into building interiors**: procedurally generated buildings, containers and searches, raid loot carried by hand [buildingmaps, search, raidloot]. A neighbourhood POI is a street of 3–5 two-storey houses on one map (rows facing a road, yards, fences, parked cars to search; ways out at both ends and the back gardens), each house's containers kept apart in the POI's ledger and the one pool falling across them; headless it is one POI searched a house at a time [buildingmaps `streetMap`, `streetHouses`, `STREET`, `streetIntro`; combat `createRaid` (upper floors' reach from every stairwell); poicont `poiHouses`; scavenge `searchHour` / `afterRaid`]. Also raid planning (entry side, scout, wait for night) [raidplan], and locks, keys and safes [locks, gunsafes]. Makeshift picks (bent wire, `GEAR.makeshiftpick`, a stack of up to 10, `CRAFTS.makeshiftpick` three a scrap at a base or in the field): pick without the crowbar and lockpicks from a lower base, as quiet, one snapping off the stack half the time an attempt; a safe, a steel security door and the gun safes are beyond them; the crowbar's picks are used first [locks `MAKESHIFT_PICK`, `picksOf`, `crowbarOf`, `makeshiftPicksOf`, `isMakeshiftPicks`, `makeshiftPickable`, `pickChance`, `wearPicks`, `openOdds` (pick.makeshift / left), `openLock`, `lockedSearchHour`; foundcars (cars), rescueraid `pickOdds` (a cell's lock); ui/locks `lockPanel` "Pick (makeshift)", ui/carpanel; `tools/makeshiftpicktest.js`].
- **Stealth**: takedowns, facing and view cones, sneaking, lights and darkness, indoor light switches [stealth, vision, indoorlight, ailights].
- **Vaulting half cover**: a person (yours, a human enemy, the third side; never the dead, nor with the arms full) goes over a waist-high cell nobody can stand on (a fence, crates, a car, sandbags, a table; not a window, cell bars, a railing or a well) at VAULT.COST tiles more than a step, never ending on it or cutting its corner, lands winded (VAULT.COST tiles less of movement for the rest of the turn, `u.vaulted` read by `moveMob`), a scuff heard nearby and VAULT.NOISE on the raid's noise; the human AI's field goes over the same cells [combat VAULT, vaultable, canVault, reachable, distField, moveAlong, moveMob; ui/isoview (the VAULT mark on the path), ui/combat (the tooltips), ui/battlehud (the WINDED chip)].
- **Windows and the kinds of door**: every window cell carries a state (`c.win = { open, lock: none | locked | fixed, known }`, rolled from the map's own dice where windows are placed: `buildingmaps.js assemble`, `combat.js genMap` / `genRaidMap`, `interior.js`): checking it is free, opening or closing it 1 action and quiet (an unlocked one from either side, a locked one from indoors only: `insideOf`), breaking it 1 action and loud (`WINDOW.BREAK`: noise, Heat, `alertNear`); a broken window ("smashed") is half cover like a closed one and nobody stands in it; a move vaults through an open or broken window at the vault's cost and crashes through a closed one (`vaultBreaks`: loud, 1–3 cuts), the dead clamber through an open or broken one (`clambers`); the raid's Climb window is quiet through an open one; a throw passes an open one (`throwBlocks`). Doors carry a kind (`c.dk`, `DOOR_KINDS`: screen, wood, front, glass, steel, security, roller, cell, barred, gate, vault) placed by archetype (`doorStyleOf`) that sets hit points, sight through a closed glass or screen door (`doorSees`, `los`), the lock's difficulty and whether it can be kicked or forced (`locks.js openOdds` by `lock.dk`), the crash of a glass door breaking and its sound (`ui/sfx-doors.js doorKind`); the dead and the human AI prefer them by their hit points on the siege field [windows, buildingmaps DOOR_KINDS, combat vaultBreaks/vaultCost/clambers/breakCell/structureName, locks; ui/combat (windowButtons, the window and door tooltips, the click on a window), ui/isoview (the SMASH tag), ui/sfx-doors (glass and screen doors, sash, glassBreak), ui/battlebar, ui/codex (Windows, Doors)].
- **Hearing and deafening** [hearing]. Ear protection: foam earplugs, muffs, electronic defenders and the team headset [data-helmets, helmets `earGearOf`], and the makeshift rag earplugs crafted from a scrap (weaker than foam, worn from a pocket under any helmet, a pair a fight) [data `ragplugs`, crafting `CRAFTS.ragplugs`, hearing `isPlugsKey` / `settleEars`].
- **Non-lethal weapons and taking people alive** (taser, stun baton, pepper spray) [nonlethal].
- **Throwables**: Molotovs, pipe bombs, flares and smoke [throwables]. **Decoys and fire**, and what the dead follow [decoys].
- **Things on the ground** and searching bodies [ground].
- **Retreat**: "Sound the retreat" on the map and break-off policies for auto-resolved fights [combat soundRetreat, breakoff].
- **Third side**: neutrals and soldiers who are neither your team nor its enemies [thirdside].
- **Siege kit**: ladders, rams and frag grenades [siege].
- **Vehicles in battle**: Humvee gunners, ramming gates [combat raidCarTurn].
- **Auto-resolve by the numbers** [combat autoResolveCombat].

## 7. Weapons, armour and gear
- **Weapons overhaul**: many real guns, melee weapons, blades and launchers [data-guns, data-blades, launchers].
- **Ammo by calibre and type**, physical magazines, rounds tracked in each gun, and a reloading bench [ammo, rounds, mags, gunload, gunrounds, reloading]. Rounds come boxed or loose. Boxes open into loose rounds, and loose rounds can be boxed again (Box, or dropped on each other) [itemstack]. **Reload sources**: where the rounds are decides a Reload's cost (mags `roundSplit` / `roundSource` / `MAGS.SOURCE_AP`, `reloadPlan`'s `from`): loose rounds or a box in a pocket or a rig pouch (`u.handBoxes`, `u.pocketBoxes`; ammo `boxesOf` lists pockets, rig, pack) 1 action, the pack both actions (a shotgun's shells too: plan `{ mode: tube | break | single, from: "pack" }`, no refusal; `getShells` stays the 1-action way to bring a handful to hand), a teammate's boxes or the team's bags both actions too (a top-up or an internal magazine: only what is at hand is quick); spare magazines unchanged (rig 1, pocket 1, pack 2). Rounds are spent at hand, pack, mates, pools (ammo `takeBattleRounds`). The AI and the raid bot prefer Get shells to a both-actions Reload (combat `aiLoad`, `packShells`); the Reload button and the weapon panel name the source (ui/combat, ui/battlehud `weaponInfo.rounds`) [mags, ammo, combat, ui/combat, ui/battlehud]. **Loading a spare magazine by hand**: in the fight's Inventory window loose rounds dropped onto one of the gun's spare magazines load it, 1 action with the rounds in a pocket or a rig pouch, both actions from the pack (mags `LOAD_AP`, `loadMagPlan`, `loadMagFrom`; combat `invAct` op "load" or a plain move onto a magazine); an empty magazine takes any type the magazine and the gun accept, one with rounds in it only more of the same; a box must be opened first; another gun's magazine is not loaded. The rounds stay where they lie (virtual rounds: a shot spends them), so a magazine fills only with rounds the gun and the quicker spares have not claimed. The magazine item's rounds badge in a fight reads the unit's entry (gunrounds `gunRounds` src "fight"; mags `magEntryAt`: the k-th magazine of a key in a place is the k-th entry, kept so by `refitKit` and a Reload's swap) [mags, combat, gunrounds, ui/inventory].
- **Gun mechanics**: fire modes, jams, suppression, over-penetration, and a damage ladder [gunfire, gunstats, ladder].
- **Attachments and gun builder**, Tarkov-style: sights, muzzles, stocks and more; factory-fitted guns; finishes and paint [gunmods, data-gunmods, partfinds, factoryfit, data-finish, ui/gunbuilder, ui/gunpaint].
- **Armour classes AC 1–6** with per-hit durability, helmets and face shields, plate carriers and rigs [armour, helmets, data-helmets, data-kit]. A rig's pouches come by kind (rifle and pistol magazines, a grenade, a radio, admin up to 0.5 kg) and every rig has **pocket pouches** that take anything a pocket takes (a chest rig and a tactical vest two, a makeshift rig and a plate carrier one) [data-kit POUCH_NAMES / SUPPLY_POUCHES, inventory pouchReason / stow / quickMove].
- **Inventory**: RPG equipment slots, pockets, packs and stacks [inventory, itemstack, stacks]. **Supplies held as items**: anything in the stores can be taken out into pockets, a pack or the off hand. It goes back into the stock when put in storage or brought home. Loot goes into pockets and packs the same way. Carried is for bulky things and whatever has no room [supitems, data-supitems, raidloot]. **Kit moves in a fight**: the battle's Inventory window (`ui/inventory peekModal`, battlebar `inventory`, Shift+B) is the acting unit's to sort on its turn: a move between pockets, pack slots, rig pouches, the hands and the off hand, or Split / Open box / Box on a stack, costs `INV_AP` (1) through `combatAct "inv" { unit, from, to, op? }` (combat invAct), and the unit's caches are refit at once (combat `refitKit`: refitUnit for the hands, boxes of rounds, each magazine's place keeping its rounds; `ensureFit` for a field battle). Armour, helmet, rig and pack stay worn [combat, ui/inventory, ui/battlebar]. **Hand over in a fight**: the battlebar `handover` order (Alt+H) trades with the player unit right next to the acting one, on the same floor, in its view cone (combat `handoverTargets`, `handoverUnfaced`, `handoverRefusal`; vision `inArc` with `VISION.HALF`): `combatAct "handover" { unit, to, from, toLoc }` (combat handoverAct) moves a thing either way between the two survivors through `inventory moveItem(…, { across: true })` for `HANDOVER_AP` (1) of the acting unit's (the mate spends nothing) and refits both with `refitKit`; a mate out of the cone gets the free Face toward them first (the button reads "Face <name>"), several in reach a chooser (`ui/combat handChooser`, `handoverCbAct`, `openHandover`, `cui.handAsk` / `cui.handReq`); the two-doll window is `ui/inventory handModal` (`handCan`, `ui.inv = { sid, peek, mate }`, `inv-mate`). The raid search window's free hand-over (raidloot `matesOf`) is unchanged [combat, inventory, vision, ui/combat, ui/inventory, ui/battlebar, ui/keymap].
- **Item icons**: every item that is not a gun has its own 16 × 16 pixel drawing on its tile, in the armory and on supply chips (guns: their own gun art) [ui/itemart, ui/itemart/*].
- **Item right-click menu**, Tarkov-style, in every inventory view: equip, reload with a round, unload, modify (the gun builder, in a fight too at an action a part), repair, salvage, eat, drink and use, with each row's cost in actions in a fight [itemacts, ui/itemmenu].
- **Item condition and repair** that costs max durability, Tarkov-style [itemcond, repair].
- **Loot distribution** by site theme and gun distribution tables [loot, gundist, gunfinds, blades].
- **Hidden item values** and worth tiers [valuation].
- **Team kit loadouts**, plus the governor buying guns [kit, govguns].

## 8. The dead, hives and hordes
