# Broken State: major features

A map of the game's major features, for agents getting oriented. Built from the header comment of every module in `prototype/src/sim` and `prototype/src/ui`, plus `prototype/README.md` (2026-10-13). The modules for each feature are in brackets: `[name]` means `prototype/src/sim/name.js`, and `[ui/name]` means `prototype/src/ui/name.js`. When you add or remove a major system, update this file too.

## 1. Campaign, world and pacing
- **Procedural State of Calder**: hex map with terrain, rivers, cities, towns, countryside sites, roads and infection [mapgen, hex, worldsize]. World sizes run Small, Normal, Large and Huge, each with its own AI group count.
- **Hourly and daily simulation tick**, seeded and deterministic [world, rng].
- **Seasons and weather**: four 28-day seasons, regional weather fronts, frozen rivers in winter that people and the dead can cross [weather, rivers].
- **Eras**: early game (years 1–4), mid game (5–9) and late game (10+), with threats that grow each year [eras].
- **Two victories**: destroy every hive, or win with the Calder Coalition (an alliance holding over half the land). "Keep playing" is available after either one [world, alliance].
- **Hardcore mode**: start on the day Calder falls with 1–4 survivors on the road, no hub, sites only half looted, and the outbreak year [hardcore*, hardcore-start, hardcore-loot, hardcore-outbreak, hardcore-groups]. When the Guard's base falls, its splinter groups and stragglers carry out the base's own stores and armory (and the strike force's remnant what the strike took out), as far as their people can carry; nothing is made for them, and the rest lies in the ruin's ground; a splinter that founds a hub has its people's kit for books [hardcore-groups militaryFalls carryOut strikeStores, loadouts grabKit, carry leaveOnGround, arsenal arsenalFromPeople]. The outbreak year has road and hub cards of its own (gridlock, Guard checkpoints and relief drops, the emergency broadcast, holdouts, looters, the newly dead; hungry people at the gate, a Guard deserter, the radio going quiet, city refugees) [events HC_EVENTS, hubevents hc_*].
- **Bare start**: every base starts with empty plots [world HUB_START].
- **New-run shortcuts**: *Random run* rolls every setting (seed, size, groups, hives, mode, starting people, zombie look, the crew and gear, Customize's points) and starts at once; *New start position* moves where you start on the same map and seed, crew kept; *Pick start on map* (the user's request 2026-10-22) opens the map and the tile you click is the start (a town for a normal hub; in Hardcore any open land that is not a hive or a group's or the Guard's base), the hover tip saying why a tile cannot be one [ui/newgame rollRandomRun, startpick startWhyNot chosenStart, main pickStartAt].
- **World configuration**: the new-run screen's *World…* window sets a run's dials (hunger, thirst, spoilage, sickness; horde size, the dead at sites, hive growth, zombie damage, infection from bites and from the land, whether the worm is curable; the AI groups' starting gear, events, raids; loot by kind; the sizes of bases, raid maps (smallest and largest; cities and towns always the largest), city crawls and hive crawls; wear, failures, fuel), with presets (Gentle, Standard, Harsh, Nightmare) that turn to Custom once a dial moves. Kept on the world (`state.worldCfg`, saved) and read where each rate is applied [worldcfg, ui/worldcfg].
- **Outbreak beyond the border**: keeps a minimum number of hives alive [outbreak].
- **Event cards and pacing**: pause rules, a card tray, auto-answers, and speeds from 1× to 5× [cardrules, ui/pacing].
- **Hub events**: home decisions every few days (a fire, a quarrel, a peddler, rats in the stores, the dead at the fence, a sick traveller, a cold snap, music at night, a wreck to strip, flu season, bad water, a coughing stranger and more) [hubevents, disease]. Also the harvest festival [festival].
- **7-day threat forecast** meter [forecast].

## 2. Survivors
- **Skills, XP, teaching, books and perks** [skills]. **Traits** with tiers and points [traits].
- **Needs**: hunger, thirst and mood gauges; eating person by person with a meal mood ladder, food preferences and ration levels [needs, eating].
- **Sleep and tiredness** [sleep].
- **Morale**: crowding, leader lift, desertion warnings and desertion [morale]. **Mental breaks** [mentalbreaks]. **Comforts and treats** [comforts].
- **Relationships**: grief, service record, memorial wall and daily mood factors [relations].
- **Bonds between survivors** (the user's request 2026-10-21: "Improve survivor inter-connected relationships and behaviors Buffs and debuffs and events"), on relations.js's one opinion a pair: named levels (rival ≤ −30, disliked ≤ −10, neutral, friendly 15+, friend 40+, close friend 70+, partner) and the few biggest reasons kept on the pair (bumpPair's `why` → bonds noteWhy, r.why; bondReason the one shown). Daily in bondsDaily at the end of relationsDaily (relRng dice). UI: the survivor screen's People list (level, opinion, reason, mentor and promise tags) and Bonds card [ui/relations], the activity line's why (bondWorkNote), the codex's People category "Bonds between survivors" [ui/codex]. AI parity: none kept for AI groups' people (ADR 0001 §3: their mood and relationships do not tick; O(n²) pairs on a Huge world); anyone who joins you is under the rules at once [bonds, bondevents; `tools/bondstest.js`].
- **Family**: couples, pregnancy, births and children growing up [family].
- **Recruits**: newcomer choice cards for strangers, refugees, loners and returning deserters [recruits].
- **Appearance and sprites**, plus survivor customization (backgrounds, customization points, starting gear, random builds) [appearance, custombg, custompoints, startgear, randomkit]. A random kit with a gun in the hands (Build, Randomize all, the gear window's dice under Customize) carries its spare magazines and a box or two of its common round in a pocket, a pouch or the pack, a box's points kept back while the other slots are drawn (the user's request 2026-10-18) [randomkit randomKit putRounds / putBox]. Every body on the battle map is a low-poly figure that faces its way and turns with the view: survivors, human enemies, the dead in every kind (the plain, the special, the armoured, the risen), the bosses, and the fallen lying along the ground; the panels show the same figures and the inventory spins one through 360°, which the player can turn by hand instead (a drag or the wheel over it stops it where it is left, a degree a pixel; ↻ Spin or a double-click sets it turning again; per figure by its spin key, so a rebuilt panel keeps the turn) [ui/figures figureSpinImg, handOf, holdFigure, dragFigure, wheelFigure, turnFigure, spinFigure, spinFrame, frameOfYaw; ui/sprites, ui/hivesprites and ui/downsprites keep the flat painters as fallbacks]. On the State Map every moving piece (your teams on foot and by car, boat or raft, the groups' parties and convoys, bands and nomads, outsiders, hordes, herds, workers on job trips) is a low-poly figure too, built from the same limb at the map's scale, facing the way it travels (every 15°, no mirroring) and animated while on its way (walk cycles, a limp, wheels turning, oxen trotting, oars pulling, the dead shuffling) [ui/mapfigures; ui/render cycleFrame and headingOf, ui/workermap; the places keep ui/mapsprites' flat painters].
- **Status icons** and a "Why did this happen?" cause chain for deaths and lost bases [status, causes].
