HD sprites: current vs redesigned, at in-game scale

Design preview only: the game is unchanged. The battle map draws a standing unit 30 art pixels tall at the chosen zoom (0.75–5 CSS px per art px; most play at 2–3). The current sprites are 22 × 26 grid pixels; the HD ones are 44 × 52 (same on-screen size, four times the pixels). Items: the inventory's rows show icons at about 24–32 CSS px. Everything is drawn with image smoothing off, like the game.
